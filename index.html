Origin story

cat origin.txt

My story began with an unhealthy addiction to video games. As a young 13 year old boy I got gifted a PC, this was very rare back then in Mumbai/India. A privilege I will never forget.

I would spend all my day fidgeting with Windows, and playing strategy and MMORPG games. Soon my brain latched onto automating level ups in these games and soon enough I found myself learning scripting and programming to hack them.

From that moment on I knew what my career would be :)

Achievement unlocked Age 13: first PC.
Then: scripting my way through level ups.
