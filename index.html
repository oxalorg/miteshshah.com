I am a Computer programmer by day, Intergalactic Rogue by night.

Origin

My story began with an unhealthy addiction to video games. As a young 13 year old boy I got gifted a PC, this was very rare back then in Mumbai/India. A privilege I will never forget. I would spend all my day fidgeting with Windows, and playing strategy and mmorpg games. Soon my brain latched onto automating level ups in these games and soon enough I found myself learning scripting and programming to hack them. From that moment on I knew what my career would be :)

Now I work as a full-stack developer, fractional CTO, software & product consultant helping startups and small to medium-sized companies figure out their product, their users, and help them with their technology stack.

Professional

I started working at Gaiwan 6 years ago as a Founding Engineer. Experienced in system architecture, data driven design, high performance computing. Also have a great radar for detecting over engineering before it happens, and a keen eye to ensure the technology work being done stays true to the business objectives. We have helped multiple clients and their teams by coming in as experts or as a fractional CTO.
